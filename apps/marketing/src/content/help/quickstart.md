title: Quickstart
description: From a product image to an exported ad in about five minutes, using the demonstration workspace.
order: 1
---

## 1. Open the workspace

Go to the app and sign in with a fixture identity, or create a demo account. Demo accounts have no passwords and nothing is emailed. The **Demo workspace** label in the header confirms you are on the simulated backend.

## 2. Create a project

Choose **New project**. Pick a template (Turntable hero is a good first one), then either select a catalog fixture or upload your own product image (PNG, JPG or WebP up to 12 MB). Add the product name and a short brief: goal, audience, tone, key message and the formats you need.

Your uploaded image is stored in your browser and shown as the reference. The 3D model built by the demo is a labelled catalog fixture, not a reconstruction of your image.

## 3. Run the workflow

Press **Run workflow**. Steps execute in order: build model, compose scene, animate, ad variants, export. Progress is measured per step (views, frames, variants). You can leave the page; the run keeps going and its status is restored when you come back.

## 4. Edit in the studio

Open the **Studio** tab. Orbit and zoom the model, click a part to select it, and change background, material colour and finish, light, camera preset, placement and the animation. Edit the headline, subheadline, call to action, brand colour, layout and aspect ratio. Every change is undoable (`⌘Z` / `Ctrl+Z`) and saved automatically.

You can also type commands in the composer at the bottom, for example `set background to charcoal`, `headline: "Glow, bottled"`, `duration 8 s` or `run the animation step`.

## 5. Review versions and export

The **Outputs** tab lists every version each run produced. Compare two, set acceptance, and select the one to use. Then **Export**: PNG snapshot and WebM preview are rendered by your browser from your edited scene; scene JSON is the editable data; the MP4 preset simulates a render queue and delivers a labelled sample clip.
